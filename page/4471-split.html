What the counts rest on

  • The export holds 427 entries but 412 ids: 15 reports come twice (overlapping export pages). One id, one report — uniqueById in causes.ts. The desk’s “412 reports” is the unique count; the raw export says 427.
  • Intake tags are guesses and disagree with the cause on 38 reports: 23 parser reports tagged hint, 15 hint reports tagged parser. Tag by tenant and channel (causeOf), not by the reporter’s browser.
  • 5 reports (desk-notes.json, oneDayMove) read as a one-day move, not a month flip. Counted under parser where they are filed, footnoted there by the desk.

Lessons

  • Merging on subject (decision 0001) put two bugs in one ticket. The body and the tenant say what the subject does not.
  • A big ticket is not one bug. Run the logic before writing “one issue, one fix queued”.
